Inbox Zero

Your agent fixed them. You get to bin them. Flick each resolved note into the bin, and mind the fan.

Streak 0 Score 0 Best 0

How to play

  • Swipe up from the note to throw it. Only the direction counts: every throw has the same arc.
  • Keys: ← → aim, Space throws.
  • The fan blows it sideways. The bigger the wind number, the more you aim into it.
  • Notes come in Notato's severities. A nit is a light scrap the fan carries off (3 points), a blocker a heavy wad that barely drifts (2 points), and minors and majors are 1 each.
  • Five in a row sets the note on fire, and burning notes score double.
  • Three misses and the run is over. The bin moves back as you bin more, then starts to slide, and the office stays late into the night.

Want something louder? Spud Cannon: knock the bug off a tower of potatoes.
