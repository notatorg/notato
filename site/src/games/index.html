Notato games

Notato turns feedback into fixes. These turn it into potatoes. Two small games for the minute while your agent works through the notes.

Spud Cannon

A bug has landed on a tower of potatoes. Knock it off with another potato. Drag back to aim, mind the wind, and try not to hit the tower.

Your agent fixed them. You get to bin them. Flick each resolved note at the bin, and aim into the fan.
