Spud Cannon

Russ is a tower of three potatoes, and there is a bug on top. Knock it off with a potato. Do not hit Russ.

Score 0 Spuds 3 Wind calm Best 0

How to play

  • Drag back anywhere on the scene, like a slingshot. The further you pull, the harder it fires. Let go to fire.
  • Keys: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, Space fires.
  • Bugs come as nits, minors, majors and blockers: the rarer and smaller, the more points. Hit one with your first spud for a bonus point.
  • Three spuds per bug. Run out, or hit the tower, and you lose a heart (and the top potato goes flying).
  • Each bug moves the tower further back, and a new one hops in every four bugs: Russ, Ruby, Sunny, Violet and Pip. Wind, the web's annoyances (cookie banners, chat pop-ups and worse) and fidgety towers join in, and the day wears on into night.
