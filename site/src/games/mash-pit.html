Mash Pit

No score, no rules, no bugs fixed. A potato launcher that never runs out, a tower of potatoes, and some buttons that make it worse.

Spuds fired 0 Kabooms 0

Make it sillier

How to play

  • Hold anywhere on the scene and potatoes fly wherever you point. Space works too, aimed with ← →.
  • Hit the bottom of the tower to send the whole thing flying. It always puts itself back together.
  • Spud rain makes it pour potatoes. Payback lets the tower have a go back.
  • Annoyance brings on a cookie banner, a support chat, a newsletter or a spinner to blow up. Towers adds another tower, up to six.
  • The bug on top can't be hit. It just laughs at you.

Want a game with a score? Spud Cannon, or Inbox Zero.
