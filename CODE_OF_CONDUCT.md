# Code of conduct

Notato follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind, assume good intent, and leave room for people who are new. It applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, tell the maintainers privately through GitHub: on the comment, issue or pull request, open the **⋯** menu, choose **Report content**, then **Report to repository admins**. Only the maintainers see the report. If it is not about something posted in the repository, open an issue that just asks a maintainer to get in touch, with no details in it.

The maintainers will look at every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and act as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
